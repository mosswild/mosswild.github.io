# Miscellaneous Workshop 3D Prints & Jigs
### Functional Shop Upgrades, Dust Collection & Sharpening Aids

> A collection of practical, shop-tested 3D printed fixtures and accessories designed to solve everyday workflow bottlenecks across woodworking, dust collection, and tool maintenance.

- **Maker Profile**: [Thingiverse (@mosswildsmade)](https://www.thingiverse.com/mosswildsmade/designs)

## 1. Parametric Magnetic 3-Inch Hose Connector

- **Thingiverse Model & F3D CAD**: [thingiverse.com/thing:6955478](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6955478)
- **The Problem**: Most off-the-shelf magnetic quick-connect dust fittings are standardized for large 4-inch central ducted systems or tiny 2.5-inch shop vac hoses. Compact tools, benchtop sanders, and portable laser enclosures frequently use **3-inch flexible ducting**, where commercial magnetic adapters are practically nonexistent.
- **Parametric F3D Model**: Built in Autodesk Fusion with parametric dimensions (`DuctDiameter`, `MagnetDiameter`, `MagnetThickness`, `WallThickness`), allowing the fitting to be scaled or adapted to custom magnet sizes.
- **Fast Tool Swapping**: Neodymium ring magnets snap securely together with an airtight friction bevel, enabling fast tool-to-hose swaps without turning screwdriver hose clamps.

## 2. Powertek T-Track End Stop

- **Thingiverse Model**: [thingiverse.com/thing:7113030](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7113030)
- **Design Heritage**: Refined from [Thingiverse #3875606](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3875606) (originally sized for Kreg T-tracks).
- **Custom Fit for Powertek**: While Kreg-style stops can physically slide into Powertek aluminum tracks, the tolerances are loose and can cant under clamping pressure. This model was reprofiled specifically for the interior groove dimensions of **Powertek T-track**.
- **Hardware**: Secures with a compact M2.5 / M3 socket head cap screw or knurled thumb knob, creating a rigid mechanical stop for repeatable crosscuts, mortising stops, and drill press fences.

![[powertek-t-track-end-stop.png]]
*Low-profile stop block designed for exact tolerances in Powertek T-tracks.*

## 3. Honing Guide for Chisels and Plane Irons (AmazonBasics)

- **Thingiverse Model**: [thingiverse.com/thing:7133690](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7133690)
- **Application**: Sharpening bench chisels and hand plane irons on diamond plates and waterstones.
- **The Upgrade**: Low-cost Eclipse-style and AmazonBasics side-clamping honing guides frequently suffer from loose roller tolerances, rough cast-iron tracking, and lack of built-in projection stops.
- **Benefits**:
  - Eliminates lateral roller wobble across the sharpening stone.
  - Ensures tool cutting edges remain square to the stone face.
  - Provides reliable projection registration for primary (25°) and secondary micro-bevel (30°) angles.
