# 3D Printed Pottery & Studio Tools
### Custom Jigs, Glaze Testing Aids & Extrusion Dies

> A collection of functional, 3D-printed tools designed in Autodesk Fusion to streamline clay forming, glaze batching, drying, and test tile organization in the ceramic studio.

- **Maker Profile**: [Thingiverse (@mosswildsmade)](https://www.thingiverse.com/mosswildsmade/designs)

---

## 1. Pottery Rim Cone

- **Model**: Pottery Rim Cone ([Thingiverse Profile ↗](https://www.thingiverse.com/mosswildsmade/designs))
- **The Problem**: Wheel-thrown or hand-built cylinders, mugs, and vases often warp out-of-round during drying or while chucking/centering on the wheel during foot trimming.
- **Key Features**:
  - **Airflow Hole**: Unlike solid commercial cones, features an open center hole that allows air to circulate inside the vessel, preventing uneven drying or moisture trapping.
  - **Stackable / Nesting**: Omits top-heavy handles so multiple cones nest compactly in studio bins.
  - **Integrated Finger Grip**: The central air hole doubles as a natural finger pull for gentle lifting without bumping delicate clay lips.

---

## 2. Interchangeable Pottery Press Molds

- **Thingiverse Model**: [thingiverse.com/thing:6655822](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6655822)
- **Application**: Slab building plates, shallow bowls, and dishes without expensive plaster bats.
- **Workflow**:
  1. Roll out an even clay slab on canvas.
  2. Lightly dust the slab with cornstarch or talc as a release agent.
  3. Place the slab over a thick block of high-density upholstery foam.
  4. Press the 3D-printed mold firmly into the foam to stretch the slab into shape.
- **Modular Handle**: Incorporates a sturdy threaded/reusable central handle that screws into different interchangeable mold heads (shallow dish, bowl, angular trays).

## 3. Talisman Sieve Mate to 8oz Jars

- **Thingiverse Model**: [thingiverse.com/thing:6776390](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6776390)
- **Application**: Small-batch glaze testing and recipe development.
- **The Problem**: Standard Talisman rotary sieves are sized for large 5-gallon studio buckets. Pouring small 100g–500g test batches through a full-size sieve into small sample jars usually results in spills and wasted materials.
- **Design**: An adapter ring that mates an **80-mesh Talisman Sieve** directly onto standard **8oz wide-mouth plastic containers**, allowing test glazes to be processed straight into labeled test jars with zero splatter.

![[talisman-sieve-mate.jpg]]
*Sieve adapter ring mating the Talisman rotary sieve directly onto 8oz plastic test jars.*

## 4. Clay Extruder 4" Die: Small Hollow Square

- **Thingiverse Model**: [thingiverse.com/thing:6751669](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6751669)
- **Application**: Wall-mounted or manual 4-inch barrel clay extruders.
- **Design**: A custom internal-bridge die designed for standard 4" round extruder barrels that produces continuous, small hollow square tubes.
- **Studio Uses**: Hollow handle stock (reducing handle weight and cracking during bisque firing), structural ceramic foot rings, and geometric test bars.

![[clay-extruder-square-die.png]]
*Internal bridge die model for extruding hollow square ceramic profile.*

## 5. Skådis Hooks for Extruded Test Tiles

- **Thingiverse Model**: [thingiverse.com/thing:7018653](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7018653)
- **Application**: Studio test tile walls and glaze reference galleries.
- **Custom Laser-Cut Skådis Boards**: 
  > [!NOTE]
  > Rather than buying standard IKEA pegboards, these boards are custom **laser cut** from draftwood/plywood using an **online Skådis pattern generator**, allowing custom board footprints tailored to available shop wall studs and shelving dimensions.
- **Hook Geometry**: Purpose-built clip profile that cradles hollow extruded test tiles securely from their underside channel, eliminating the need to poke fragile mounting holes into wet greenware.

![[skadis-test-tile-hooks.jpeg]]
*Extruded glaze test tiles mounted securely using 3D printed hooks on laser-cut Skådis board.*

## 6. Test Tile Rack Mold

- **Thingiverse Model**: [thingiverse.com/thing:6746133](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6746133)
- **Application**: Casting reusable kiln-stand firing racks for runny or melting glaze tests.
- **Method**: Allows casting groggy, high-temperature cone 10 clay stands to elevate cone 6 test tiles during firings, preventing runny glaze runs from damaging kiln shelves.
