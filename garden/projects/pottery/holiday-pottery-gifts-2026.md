# Holiday Pottery Studio (Christmas 2026)

## Overview

A dedicated sprint to produce a cohesive collection of functional and sculptural ceramic holiday gifts. Designed around low-friction weekend studio blocks to balance wheel work, mold iterations, and hand-building without cluttering the garage.

---

## The Pieces

### 1. Coffee Pour-Over Set
- **Method:** Wheel-thrown
- **Components:**
  - **Pour-Over Cone:** Thrown body with carved interior filter channels/fluting and a pierced drip drain. Will need a wide lip to sit over mugs or jug.
  - **Matching Coffee Mug:** Thrown cylinder with a pulled, ergonomic strap handle joined at leather-hard.
  - **Matching Coffee Jug**: Kind of like a pitcher for the coffee to make a large batch with which the cone will sit over.
- **Finishing:** Food-safe interior liner glaze paired with an earthy exterior glaze; designed to pair with local Pacific Northwest specialty coffee.

I'm going to start with 650 grams for throwing this (OldForge uses 550g). I have a V60 guide I laser cut that I will use. 
Make the disc curve upward. 

I'll start the session by throwing 2 cylinders with practice clay.

Throw with white clay with speckles mixed in. G-Mix6 shrinkage ~11%.

---

### 2. Aquarium Props & Moai Planter
- **Method:** Sculptural Hand-Building
- **Components:**
  - **Moai-Style Planter:** Slab/coil construction featuring carved brow, nose, and lips, complete with drainage.
  - **Aquarium Caves & Sculptures:** Solid sculpted and hollowed out with generous vent and pass-through holes for tank water circulation.
- **Finishing:** 
  - Planter: Weather-resistant exterior stoneware glaze.
  - Aquarium Props: Left unglazed and vitrified to Cone 5/6 to keep tank water chemistry inert and safe for aquatic life.

---

### 3. Napkin Holder & Pond Sculpture / Planter
- **Method:** Stiff-Slab & Hand-Building
- **Components:**
  - **Napkin Holder:** Flat-rolled slabs, beveled edges, joined at stiff leather-hard for crisp geometric lines.
  - **Pond Sculpture / Planter:** Hand-built exterior stoneware form with uniform wall thickness designed for garden/pondside weather resilience.
- **Finishing:** Durable satin or semi-matte outdoor glaze resistant to thermal shock.

---

### 4. Mold-Formed Dinnerware Set
- **Project:** Matching Blates & Bowls
- **Method:** Slab-built with plaster drape/slump forms
- **Components:**
  - Blates (shallow rim bowl-plates)
  - Round bowls
  - Square bowls
- **Workflow:** Cyclical slab pressings (utilizing the 3 plaster molds in staggered 60–90 minute water-wicking sessions).
- **Finishing:** Durable, scratch-resistant food-safe glaze across all functional eating surfaces.

| **08. Pack & Wrap** | Dec 19 – Dec 20 | Diamond hand-pad foot sanding, final inspection, gift wrapping |

## Studio Principles & Notes

- **Friction Reduction:** Set up the tablet/screen stand with an episode queued before getting clay on the hands.
- **Venting Rule:** Double-check every closed sculptural cavity on the caves and pond sculpture before the bisque load to eliminate steam trap hazards.
- **Bare Clay Integrity:** Keep all underwater ceramic pieces completely raw and fully vitrified to protect tank pH and livestock.
