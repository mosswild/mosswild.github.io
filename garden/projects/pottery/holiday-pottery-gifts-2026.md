# Holiday Pottery Studio (Christmas 2026)

## Overview

A dedicated sprint to produce a cohesive collection of functional and sculptural ceramic holiday gifts. Designed around low-friction weekend studio blocks to balance wheel work, mold iterations, and hand-building without cluttering the garage.

---

### 2. Moai-Style Planter
- **Method:** Sculptural Hand-Building (slab / coil construction)
- **Components:**
  - Carved facial features (prominent brow, elongated nose, stylized lips).
  - Bottom drainage hole for healthy root aeration.
- **Finishing:** Weather-resistant exterior stoneware glaze; suitable for indoor or patio display.

---

### 3. Aquarium Caves & Props
- **Method:** Sculptural Hand-Building (solid sculpted, then hollowed)
- **Components:**
  - Organic cave shelters hollowed out to uniform wall thickness.
  - Generous pass-through tunnels and top vent holes to promote tank water flow and prevent stagnant pockets.
- **Finishing:** Completely unglazed and vitrified to Cone 5/6 to keep tank water chemistry inert and safe for aquatic life.
	- If glazed used only white or red glaze

### 5. Pond Sculpture / Planter
- **Method:** Hand-Building (coils & slabs)
- **Components:**
  - Exterior stoneware form designed for garden, patio, or pondside accents.
  - Uniform wall thickness and generous hollow venting for thermal resilience.
- **Finishing:** Durable satin or semi-matte outdoor glaze resistant to thermal shock.

---

### 6. Mold-Formed Dinnerware Set
- **Project:** Matching Blates & Bowls
- **Method:** Slab-built with plaster drape/slump forms
- **Components:**
  - Blates (shallow rim bowl-plates)
  - Round bowls
  - Square bowls
- **Workflow:** Cyclical slab pressings (utilizing the 3 plaster molds in staggered 60–90 minute water-wicking sessions).
- **Finishing:** Durable, scratch-resistant food-safe glaze across all functional eating surfaces.
	- Add coil of clay during the pre-leather hard stage (after drying like an hour or two) to make the feet.
- **Glazes:** The matte white, the green, the denim blue, and the red are all good.

### 7. Self-Draining Soap Dish
- **Method:** Slab-built (drape mold or stiff-slab with carved drainage)
- **Components:**
  - Single slab that is folded to an angle and a pour spout added.
- **Finishing:** Smooth, easy-to-clean satin or gloss glaze resistant to soap scum buildup; waxed foot for bare vitrified contact points.

	- White glaze

## Production Timeline

| Phase | Window | Focus / Milestones |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01. Wheel** | Oct 31 – Nov 1 | Throw pour-over cone & mug; trim, carve ribs, attach handle |
| **02. Slabs & Molds** | Nov 7 – Nov 8 | Roll slabs, run dinnerware mold cycles 1 & 2; assemble napkin holder & soap dishes |
| **03. Sculpting** | Nov 14 – Nov 15 | Hand-build Moai planter & aquarium props; shape pond sculpture/planter |
| **04. Drying** | Nov 21 – Nov 22 | Seam cleanup, edge sponging, verify hollow vents, slow rack drying |
| **05. Bisque** | Nov 28 – Nov 29 | Pre-heat / candle cycle, Cone 04/06 bisque firing |
| **06. Glaze Prep** | Dec 5 – Dec 6 | Wax resist foot rings, spray/dip glazes (keep aquarium props bare) |
| **07. Glaze Fire** | Dec 12 – Dec 13 | High-fire Cone 5/6 glaze kiln run |
| **08. Pack & Wrap** | Dec 19 – Dec 20 | Diamond hand-pad foot sanding, final inspection, gift wrapping |

---

## Studio Principles & Notes

- **Friction Reduction:** Set up the tablet/screen stand with an episode queued before getting clay on the hands.
- **Venting Rule:** Double-check every closed sculptural cavity on the caves and pond sculpture before the bisque load to eliminate steam trap hazards.
- **Bare Clay Integrity:** Keep all underwater ceramic pieces completely raw and fully vitrified to protect tank pH and livestock.
