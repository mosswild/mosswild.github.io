# Filming Gear & Video Rig

> An overview of the cameras, mobile rigs, and action setups used to document pottery, workshop builds, outdoor rides, and vlog updates.

---

## Current Setup

### 1. Panasonic Lumix DC-G95 (Primary Mirrorless)
- **Role**: Primary camera for workbench demonstrations, studio shots, talking-head segments, and detailed macro b-roll.
- **Recent Upgrade**: Stepped up from the trusty Lumix G85. Brings a 20.3 MP sensor without an optical low-pass filter, pre-installed V-Log L for wider dynamic range and color grading, unlimited recording limits, and improved ergonomics.
- **Wireless Control & Geotagging**: Features onboard Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), which inspired the [[projects/software/garmin-camcmd|CamCommand]] project to create a phone-free wrist remote and GPS logger on the Garmin Instinct 3 Solar.

### 2. Apple iPhone 15 (Everyday & Run-and-Gun)
- **Role**: High-mobility capture, quick workbench clips, vertical video, and run-and-gun b-roll when setting up a mirrorless rig is impractical.
- **Recent Upgrade**: Upgraded from an iPhone 11. While it sits about three generations behind the bleeding-edge flagship release, moving from the 11 to the 15 is a dramatic generational leap:
  - **Sensors & Optics**: 48 MP primary sensor with 2x optical-quality sensor crop zoom.
  - **Stabilization & Video Modes**: Action mode and Cinematic mode offer smooth handheld tracking without a gimbal.
  - **I/O & Workflow**: USB-C port allows direct connection to external SSDs, microphones, and faster desktop transfers compared to legacy Lightning cables.
  - **Performance**: Significant dynamic range, low-light, and color fidelity improvements over the 11.

### 3. GoPro Hero 8 Black (Action & Motion)
- **Role**: Walking clips, biking POV, outdoor motion, and harsh/dusty environment capture.
- **Stabilization**: HyperSmooth 2.0 provides solid horizon leveling and stabilization without complex physical gimbals.
- **Shop Dual-Use**: Also pairs with custom 3D-printed workshop mounts, including the [[projects/workshop/snapmaker-artisan-mods#2-bed-mounted-gopro-timelapse-mount|Bed-Mounted GoPro Timelapse Mount]] on the Snapmaker Artisan to capture jitter-free additive prints and CNC cuts locked directly to the moving bed frame.

## The Legacy Workhorses (Archive Footage)

Most existing project archives, past build logs, and legacy b-roll were captured on the previous long-running combination:

- **Panasonic Lumix G85**: The backbone mirrorless body for years. Its reliable 5-axis Dual I.S. 2 and articulated flip-out screen made it a reliable workshop documentation tool, even with its 16 MP sensor and older contrast-detect autofocus.
- **iPhone 11**: Handled casual shop observations, process photos, and rapid social clips for years before retiring to backup duty.
