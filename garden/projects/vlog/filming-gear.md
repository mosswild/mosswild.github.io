# Filming Gear

Cameras and devices used for filming and recording footage.

---

## Current Gear

- **Panasonic Lumix G95**: Upgraded from the Lumix G85 as the primary camera. Connects over BLE with [[projects/software/garmin-camcmd|CamCommand]].
- **iPhone 15**: Upgraded from the iPhone 11. While it is about three generations behind the newest model, it is a significant upgrade over the 11.
- **GoPro Hero 8**: Action camera used occasionally for walking or biking footage, as well as on the [[projects/workshop/snapmaker-artisan-mods#2-bed-mounted-gopro-timelapse-mount|bed-mounted timelapse fixture]] on the Snapmaker Artisan.
