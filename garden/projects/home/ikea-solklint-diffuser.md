# IKEA Solklint Lamp Diffuser
### Custom Shade Insert for Glare-Free Ambient Illumination

> A 3D-printed internal lamp shade diffuser designed to tame harsh, direct bulb glare from the IKEA Solklint brass and fluted glass light fixture.

- **Thingiverse Model**: [thingiverse.com/thing:7287216](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7287216)
- **Maker Profile**: [Thingiverse (@mosswildsmade)](https://www.thingiverse.com/mosswildsmade/designs)

---

## The Problem

The IKEA Solklint series (pendant and table lamps) features clear, fluted glass paired with brass accents. While visually striking, the transparency of the glass shade causes distinct lighting issues:
1. **Harsh Direct Glare**: Standard clear or frosted E26/E12 LED bulbs create an uncomfortably bright hotspot directly in the line of sight.
2. **Aesthetic Limitations**: Unless paired with expensive, dim decorative filament Edison bulbs, standard utilitarian household LED bulbs look unfinished through the clear fluting.
3. **Tolerances in Previous Models**: Existing third-party diffusers on Printables and Thingiverse often fail to seat correctly due to subtle dimension and collar revisions IKEA made across Solklint production batches.

---

## The Design & Printing

- **Geometry**: Engineered with precise internal diameters to friction-fit securely around the central brass socket mount inside the glass shade.
- **Light Transmission**:
  - Softens the harsh direct beam into a gentle, 360-degree ambient room glow.
  - Preserves the decorative refraction patterns through the outer fluted glass without burning eyes.
- **Material Selection**:
  - **Natural / Translucent PETG or Heat-Resistant White PLA**: PETG is strongly recommended for its higher glass transition temperature ($T_g \approx 75^\circ\text{C}$), ensuring the insert will not sag or warp under continuous convective heat from the LED bulb base.
  - **Infill / Perimeters**: 2 perimeters with 0% or low gyroid infill creates an even, cloud-like diffusion texture without dark banding.
