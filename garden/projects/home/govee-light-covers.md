# Govee Light Covers & Cable Housing

Custom 3D printed mounting covers designed for Govee permanent/outdoor lights that integrate directly with cable raceway housing for clean mounting and future paint matching.

- **Thingiverse Model & Files**: [thingiverse.com/thing:7294564](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7294564)
- **Maker Profile**: [thingiverse.com/mosswildsmade/designs](https://www.thingiverse.com/mosswildsmade/designs)

---

## The Problem

Stock Govee lights and mounting brackets leave exposed wiring between light nodes, and standard adhesive clips don't provide a clean, architectural finish. Additionally, exposed plastic and wires contrast against trim or soffits, and aren't easily paintable without risking lens coverage.

---

## Design Goals & Approach

1. **Cable Raceway Integration**:
   - Designed the cover geometry to interface flush with standard wire raceway/cable housing.
   - Conceals all connecting wire slack between lights inside continuous channels.

2. **Paintability**:
   - Engineered so the covers and raceways can be painted to match trim/soffit colors in the future without disassembling the lights.
   - Clean lens cutout masking or recessed lip to protect the LED emitters during painting.

3. **Secure Mounting**:
   - Snug retention for the Govee light modules.
   - Mechanical screw mounting to avoid reliance on outdoor adhesive tape failure over seasonal temperature swings.
