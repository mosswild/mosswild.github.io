## The Problem

Stock Govee outdoor light installations suffer from two common aesthetic and durability drawbacks:
1. **Exposed Wire Slack**: Bare wiring runs between nodes with sticky adhesive clips that contrast against soffits and degrade across seasons.
2. **Bulky, Unfinished Power Brick**: The large black power brick dangling near outlets looks unintegrated and cannot be easily painted or concealed.

---

## 1. Light Node Covers & Raceway Housing

- **Thingiverse Model**: [Thingiverse #7294564](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7294564)
- **Raceway Integration**: The node covers interface flush with standard wire raceway/cable channels, concealing all wire slack between nodes inside clean, straight runs.
- **Paint Matching**: Engineered so the covers and raceways can be spray-painted or brush-painted to match fascia/soffit trim without getting paint on the LED lenses.
- **Mechanical Mounting**: Screw-mounted bases ensure the lighting remains secure without relying on outdoor adhesive tape during freezing winters or summer heat.

## 2. Latching Power Brick Holder

- **Thingiverse Model & F3D**: [Thingiverse #7164014](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7164014)
- **Swinging Latch Mechanism**: Features a hinged, swinging enclosure with a snap latch, allowing the power brick to be serviced, unplugged, or inspected without unscrewing the mount from the wall.
- **Parametric F3D Model**: Designed in Autodesk Fusion 360 with fully customizable parameters (`Length`, `Width`, `Depth`, `WallThickness`) to adapt to different Govee power brick revisions or third-party 12V/24V power supplies.
- **Covered Outdoor Usage**: The enclosure is designed for neatness and paint concealment rather than a hermetic seal. It is **not waterproof** and is intended for covered patios, under-soffit alcoves, garages, or weather-shielded outlets.
- **Paintable Exterior**: Matches exterior siding or stucco textures when primed and painted.

## 3D Printing & Material Guidelines

- **Recommended Filament**: 
  - **PETG** or **ASA / ABS** for outdoor UV resistance and resistance to thermal warping under direct sunlight.
  - Avoid standard PLA for outdoor installations due to UV breakdown and low glass transition temperature (~55°C).
- **Print Settings**:
  - 3–4 perimeter walls for mechanical screw strength.
  - 20%+ gyroid or grid infill.
  - Orient hinge pins and latches along layer lines for maximum shear strength.
