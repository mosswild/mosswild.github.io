# Patio Half-Barrel Pond & Aquatic Ecosystem
### Micro-Pond Setup, Marginal Plantings & Custom Rim Planters

> An outdoor micro-pond project built inside a reclaimed half-barrel, featuring biological filtration, marginal bog plants, and custom parametric 3D-printed hanging rim planters.

## Project Overview

A half-barrel pond (typically made from a cut oak whiskey or wine barrel fitted with a rigid or flexible pond liner) creates a self-contained, low-maintenance aquatic ecosystem on a deck or patio.

The goals for this barrel pond build:
1. **Lush Marginal Greenery**: Establish marginal bog and emergent plants that grow out of the water to soften the wooden rim.
2. **Natural Biofiltration**: Use plant root systems to strip excess nitrates and nutrients from the water column, preventing algae blooms without harsh chemical treatments.
3. **Wildlife & Sound**: Provide gentle water movement and an inviting water feature for birds, pollinators, and beneficial insects.

## Parametric Hanging Pond & Aquarium Planter

- **Thingiverse Model**: [thingiverse.com/thing:7358797](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7358797)
- **The Challenge**: Standard commercial aquatic plant pots sit at the bottom of the pond, requiring deep cinder blocks or bricks to raise marginal plants up to the water surface. Furthermore, finding planters that securely hook over the thick, tapered staves of a wooden half-barrel is nearly impossible.
- **Parametric F3D Architecture**:
  - Fully modeled in Autodesk Fusion with exposed user parameters.
  - Key customizable dimensions:
    - `RimThickness`: Adjusts the hook bracket to fit anything from thin 6mm aquarium glass to 35mm thick wooden barrel staves.
    - `BasketWidth` & `BasketDepth`: Customizes plant volume for shallow groundcovers vs. deep-rooted bog irises.
    - `SlitWidth` & `Spacing`: Controls root aeration and water flow through the soil basket while retaining pea gravel or pond substrate.
- **Printing Recommendations**: Printed in **PETG** or **ASA** to withstand continuous UV exposure and outdoor water immersion without leaching harmful chemicals or softening in summer sun.

## Planting & Substrate Strategy

- **Root Placement**: Plants are potted in aquatic mesh baskets using inert volcanic rock, pond pea gravel, or aquatic planting clay rather than organic potting soil.
- **Recommended Marginal Plants**:
  - *Blue Flag Iris* / *Japanese Sweet Flag* (vertical architectural contrast)
  - *Creeping Jenny* (*Lysimachia*) (cascades over the outer barrel rim)
  - *Water Mint* or *Horsetail* (vigorous nutrient uptake)
