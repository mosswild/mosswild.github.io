# Year-Round Holiday & Accent Lighting
### Permanent Multi-Season Installation with Govee Christmas Lights 2

> Planning a permanent exterior lighting installation using Govee Christmas Lights 2 and outdoor Command strip hangers, designed to serve as customizable accent lighting for every holiday and season throughout the year.

- **Lighting Hardware:** [Govee Christmas Lights 2 (66 ft)](https://a.co/d/0fXxxFG7)
- **Mounting Hardware:** [Outdoor Command Strip Christmas Light Hangers](https://a.co/d/076bROeN)
- **Related Project:** [[projects/home/govee-light-covers|Govee Outdoor Lights: Node Covers & Power Brick Enclosure]]

---

## Concept & Objectives

The goal is to replace temporary seasonal setups with a single, permanent installation that remains discreet during the day and fully programmable at night. Rather than taking lights down each January, RGBIC control allows shifting palettes across any holiday (Christmas, Halloween, 4th of July) or setting warm, understated architectural lighting year-round.

- **Coverage:** 66 ft strand provides adequate length for the front porch rim, exterior stair stringer, and accent runs.
- **Fastening:** Damage-free outdoor Command clips. A surplus of clips allows tight spacing to maintain crisp lines and prevent sagging across seasons.

### Planned Runs

1. From the plug near the door, run to the outside upper portion of deck.
2. Wrap around and along the diagonal of the staircase.
3. Come back up the staircase on the inside (so it lights up one side of the treads) and comes back around the inside.
4. From there (at the black part of the line) I may drill into the deck and have the lights go under the slats. This is to cross the entry area of the porch without causing a tripping hazard and as an added benefit, this may provide lighting to the under-deck area.
5. Come back up the right side of the entry path and line the base of the porch.
6. Line the diagonal side of the neighbor's bannister.
7. Line the inside of the stairwell area (again lighting up the treads)
8. Wrap around the outside and line the outer section.
9. If there is extra line, either come down the corner or wrap the shrub out in the front depending on how much extra is left.

---

## Installation Considerations

- **Surface Prep:** Clean siding and railings thoroughly with isopropyl alcohol before adhering outdoor Command strips to maximize long-term bonding against rain and cold.
- **Clip Spacing:** Use close clip spacing at direction changes and along horizontal runs to keep the strand taut and prevent line droop. The command strips say every 2 ft. so I should be good.
- **Power & Controller Routing:** Position the lead wire and controller in a sheltered location near the exterior outlet.
